# summary
our app, cartmates, is exclusive to our university which serves as a platform to find buying partners. there are some offers where you can save buy ordering in higher quantity; but you don't need that much, then you can team up with someone who need that, and order as a group. our slogan is: buy together, save together.

# member status vs campaign status (important distinction, applies everywhere below)
campaign status = lifecycle of the whole campaign (recruiting, ordered, etc, see status system section).
member status = where an individual person stands inside a campaign. three states:
- interested: expressed interest, no payment made, doesn't count toward qty cap. only exists once qty cap is already full.
- hold: paid, payment screenshot uploaded, waiting on host verification. counts toward qty cap.
- confirmed: host verified the payment. counts toward qty cap.
so cap progress = confirmed + hold. interested people sit outside the cap, waiting for a slot.

there will be a onboarding with three detail cards as hey save on product cost, save on delivery, join student community etc random intro to our app. with a regiser button, login button. also to skip and use as a guest.
### guest
they can only browse, not create any group or join or anything.
### already user

a form with their university registration no., and username and password. this user will be in pending and can browse like a guest. an admin or student volunteer will approve his account by actually verifying its him. as its exclusive for one campus it will be the easiest method.

## home screen or catalog
this will show campaigns. this will highlight rows with products such as campaigns close to reaching their qty target (needs 15 qty total, already have 12 between confirmed+hold), campaigns ending soon (time), campaigns with maximum savings in percentage. and then grid scroll of ai suggested items user might like. they can select top 5 categories as floating buttons before this grid.
this page will also have a search bar. also a button for a popup of all categories to select from.

## bottom navigation
home, my groups, create, notifications, account

## create campaign screen
step 1: pick campaign type - same product or same vendor. two big simple tappable cards.

### same product form
image upload, product name (this is the campaign name), category, source (url or address), original price without group, price tiers (add tier: at X total qty reached, price per unit is Y - repeatable, tiers key off total qty not headcount), minimum qty to proceed, qty cap (host sets this, can raise it later from active group management), deadline date/time, pickup spot (text field, e.g. "meet at library gate"), campaign description (simple text block, why join / savings explanation), additional details box.

no member-count field at creation - qty is the only limit mechanism. participant headcount is just derived/displayed, never something the host sets directly.

### same vendor form
title (vendor name or a specific ongoing offer, e.g. "aliexpress bundle deal"), vendor logo/thumbnail image, source/url, original price context, deadline (default and usually the only proceed condition), optional toggle "set a quantity target" - off by default, switch on only if the vendor deal actually needs a qty threshold (like an aliexpress bundle), which reveals a target qty field. pickup spot, campaign description (simple text block), additional details box. no price tiers here since price is resolved per-member at approval.

## campaign detail screen
### campaign type: same product
image, name, host name, price per unit at current tier. category
without group original price.
qty progress: minimum qty to proceed, current qty (confirmed + hold), qty cap. small secondary line: participant counts (confirmed / hold / interested), kept less prominent than the qty numbers.
price tiers list: at X qty reached, price becomes Y (repeat).

deadline / countdown

updates section: one-way timestamped announcements from host only (e.g. "order placed today", "items arrived, pickup tomorrow 5pm"). not a status change, just a log.
additional detail box where host add other needed informations as needed.

host reputation: name, image, quick summary - tapping opens reputation detail screen

a sticky banner at bottom showing deadline countdown and qty progress. button label depends on member state: "join campaign" (qty cap not yet full) or "mark as interested" (qty cap full - no payment needed to express interest, host decides later if it matters, and this person gets auto-notified if a slot frees up).

#### join form for same product
qty he wants to buy, and he will see a payment detail, he will make payment, and add screenshot of that payment. he will be marked as hold, and this payment verification will be passed to host. if the qty cap is already full when they try to join, this form is skipped entirely and they instead go through mark-as-interested (no payment, just a confirm).

### campaign type: same vendor
vendor logo or offer thumbnail image, host name. why this campaign. deadline, pickup spot, additional informations. optional qty target progress if the host enabled it at creation, otherwise just deadline countdown. members and other generic.

#### join form for same vendor
repeatable product line item: image of what product you want to buy, how much, additional instruction - with a "+ add another product" button to request more than one item in the same submission. each line item goes to host for individual approval (some items in one submission might be available, others not). after per-item approval i see payment detail per approved item, make payment, add screenshot, marked as hold, host verifies, then confirmed.

## my groups screen
segmented toggle: passive (campaigns i'm in) / active (campaigns i host). horizontal scrollable status pills at top of the list for quick filtering. floating status filter buttons can be dropped in favor of the pills - pills do the same job faster.

### passive group screen
list of campaigns i'm involved in - my qty (if hold/confirmed) or just "interested" tag, my member status badge (interested / hold / confirmed), short campaign status info. horizontal status pills to filter.
tapping a confirmed/hold campaign opens the order status screen. tapping an interested campaign opens the regular campaign detail screen (nothing to track yet, they haven't actually joined).

### passive group detail / order status screen
opens when tapping a hold/confirmed campaign from passive list. shows a simple vertical timeline of status changes and host updates combined. host contact info. a "confirm receipt" button, shown once status is arrived/ready-for-pickup, tapped by member once they've physically gotten their item.

### active group screen
list of campaigns i am hosting. horizontal status pills to filter.

### active group management screen
opens when tapping a campaign from active list. host can: raise the qty cap (non-destructive), other non-destructive edits, post one-way updates (see updates section above), change campaign status, add tracking number/link once ordered.
member list with three tabs: confirmed / hold / interested.
- hold tab: approve or reject payment screenshot. rejecting immediately frees a slot and auto-notifies the interested pool that a slot opened.
- interested tab: just a waiting list, host can see who's watching, no action required unless a slot frees up (system handles the notify).

## campaign status system
forward path: recruiting -> ordered -> arrived/ready for pickup -> completed
failure branches off recruiting:
- failed - not enough qty/members: deadline hit, minimum not reached. no host fault, no penalty, auto-trigger refund flow for any hold-member payments already made.
- failed - host fault: minimum was reached but host didn't deliver/order/disappeared. triggers dispute -> refunded flow. penalty applies per reputation rules.
cancelled: host manually pulls the campaign before it's ordered, discretionary, rare case.
updates (see above) are separate from status - they don't change status, just log announcements on the timeline.
reminder: this is campaign-level status, separate from member-level status (interested/hold/confirmed) described earlier.

## notifications screen
simple list view of system alerts - new member joined, payment verified/rejected, a slot opened and you're on the interested list, status changed, host posted an update, deadline approaching, dispute resolved, etc.

## account screen
trimmed down now that my groups owns the campaign lists. this screen is just: profile picture, name, editable fields. statistics section: two simple metric cards showing "total money saved" and "total campaigns joined". reputation summary (tap through to reputation detail screen).

## reputation detail screen
header: profile photo, name, role badge (regular/cr/admin), reputation score.
two stat rows: campaigns hosted/completed, campaigns joined.
simple chronological history list of past campaigns with a status icon each.

# payment trust model
payment happens directly to host at join time (member pays, uploads screenshot, host verifies, member marked hold then confirmed). interested members don't pay anything upfront. exclusive-campus model keeps this low-friction and personally accountable - each member is personally verified at registration. if a campaign fails (either failure type above), host is expected to refund hold/confirmed member payments; host-fault failures route through dispute.

# reputation
1. if host fails to order on time but had reached the minimum qty/members (failed - host fault), then for each confirmed member one point penalty.
2. for each successful order (completed status), one point for each confirmed member.
3. unless one has successfully disputed all issues such as refunding failed campaigns or anything scammy, he cant neither create any other campaigns nor participate in any campaigns.
this is on business logic rather than ui ux, just keep in mind reputation numbers need to show up on host reputation summary (campaign detail) and reputation detail screen.

# empty states
keep minimal - icon + one line of text is enough for any empty list (no campaigns joined, no notifications, no history). don't over-design these.
